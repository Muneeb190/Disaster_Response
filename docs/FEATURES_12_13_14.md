# ResQNet triage features

## Smart follow-up

Before submission, the reporter may receive one short, skippable question for the most important unknown fact. The question and answer are stored with the report.

## Emergency risk score

Risk is calculated from trapped people, injuries, vulnerable people, worsening conditions, and the number affected. Unknown facts remain explicitly unknown. Independent nearby reports add a capped corroboration bonus.

## Information confidence

Confidence is separate from risk. It reflects location quality, known facts, description detail, follow-up answers, coordinator verification, and independent nearby reports. High-risk, low-confidence incidents are marked for urgent checking.

## Duplicate reports and combined incidents

Reports are compared by incident type, location, time, and description similarity. Coordinators can combine likely duplicates into a primary incident without deleting the original reports, and can separate them later. Highly similar reports may fill facts that are still unknown on the primary report.

## Testing

No shared credentials or preloaded incidents are stored in the repository. Create users through signup and administrator invitations, then submit reports through the application.
